# About Me
I am a rising fourth year PhD student in statistics at [Texas A&M University](https://stat.tamu.edu/) working in the [Katzfuss group](https://sites.google.com/view/katzfuss/). I am interseted in Bayesian optimziation, approximate posterior inference and Gaussian process approximations.  

I completed a combined B.S./M.S. in Applied Math from [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/) in May 2018. After finshing at CU I worked in the [Statistical Engineering Division](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed) at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). During the summer of 2022 I was a research intern at Microsoft Research New England. In the summer of 2023 I will be a Research Intern with Toyota Research Insitute in Los Altos, CA.
