# About Me

I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon. I earned my PhD in Statistics from the University of Wisconsin–Madison, where I worked with [Professor Matthias Katzfuss](https://sites.google.com/view/katzfuss/). My doctoral research focused on Bayesian optimization, uncertainty quantification, and approximate posterior inference.

I completed a combined B.S./M.S. in Applied Mathematics at the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/). Before starting my PhD, I worked in the [Statistical Engineering Division](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed) at NIST. During graduate school, I also interned at Microsoft Research New England and Toyota Research Institute.
