# About Me
I am a fifth year PhD student in statistics at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison]([https://stat.tamu.edu/](https://stat.wisc.edu/)) working in the [Katzfuss group](https://sites.google.com/view/katzfuss/) (formerly at [Texas A&M University](https://stat.tamu.edu/)). I am interseted in Bayesian optimization, uncertainty quantification, approximate posterior inference and Gaussian process approximations.  

I completed a combined B.S./M.S. in Applied Math from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/) in May 2018. After CU Boulder I worked in the [Statistical Engineering Division](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed) at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). During the summer of 2022 I was a research intern at Microsoft Research New England. In the summer of 2023 I was a Research Intern at Toyota Research Insitute in Los Altos, CA.
