# About Me
I am a second year PhD student in statistics at [Texas A&M University](https://stat.tamu.edu/). I work on scalable Bayesian optimziation with professor [Matthias Katzfuss](https://sites.google.com/view/katzfuss/). 

I completed a combined B.S./M.S. in Applied Math from [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/) in May 2018. After finshing at CU I worked in the [Statistical Engineering Division](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed) at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).


# Research Interests
Bayesian Statistics, Bayesian optimization, and Gaussian process approximation.
