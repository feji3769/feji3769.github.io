# About Me
I am a first year statistics PhD student at [Texas A&M University](https://stat.tamu.edu/). I completed a combined B.S./M.S. in Appplied Math from [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/) in May 2018. After finshing at CU I worked in the [Statistical Engineering Division](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed) at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). Please reach out if you are interested in collaborating or just want to learn more about my work.


# Research Interests
Bayesian Statistics, Generative Modeling, Distributed Computing and Machine Learning.
