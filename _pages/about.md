# About Me
I am a rising third year PhD student in statistics at [Texas A&M University](https://stat.tamu.edu/) advised by [Matthias Katzfuss](https://sites.google.com/view/katzfuss/). Currently I am interseted in Bayesian optimziation, approximate posterior inference and Gaussian process approximations.  

I completed a combined B.S./M.S. in Applied Math from [University of Colorado Boulder](https://www.colorado.edu/amath/) in May 2018. After finshing at CU I worked in the [Statistical Engineering Division](https://www.nist.gov/itl/sed) at the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). During the summer of 2022 I am working as a research intern at Microsoft Research New England.
